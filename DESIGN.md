# GradeKade interface

An operating workspace, grounded in the precision of a teacher's marked register: warm white paper, ink navy text, a restrained dark green action color, amber for exceptions, red for incorrect decisions. No marketing hero, decorative charts, animations or elaborate design system.

Persistent compact left navigation on desktop; wrapped top navigation on mobile. Task headings and one primary action, plain statistical strips, dense but legible tables, large worksheet/crop areas. Review is a focused two-column desk: handwriting first, decision controls adjacent. Numeric data is tabular. Forms have explicit labels; errors explain recovery; all focus states visible. Use a self-hosted readable sans face. Keep geometric region overlays visually distinct from worksheet content.

Design choices inferred from the detailed brief under the user's explicit delegation of routine decisions.

## Mobile scanning and booklet review
Dedicated `/scan` and `/booklets` task surfaces inherit paper/ink/green. Phone capture carries student/template choices across pages, a large framing/preview area, ordered thumbnails and an explicit submit action. Mobile review keeps large crops above fixed thumb controls; desktop uses the same judgments beside the crop. Capture quality remains a manual check, and synthetic OCR must be labeled on every demo result.
