# GradeKade

## Product
AI-assisted arithmetic worksheet grading for high-volume tutoring centers. Paper stays; a grader resolves ambiguous answers quickly. Success is less human time per worksheet at high auto-grade precision.

## Users and workflow
Center administrators configure blank templates once. Employees scan ordered batches at a grading desk on a phone, then resolve exceptions on the same device or a desktop. Phone scanning and rapid review are core workflows. Desktop adds template configuration, bulk review and administration. Existing curriculum and human educational judgment stay in place.

## Platform
web

Mobile first for scanning and quick review; desktop for configuration and bulk operations. No native app commitment.

## Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind; Python/FastAPI; PostgreSQL; OpenCV/Pillow. Explicitly selected by user.

## Constraints
Elementary arithmetic only; exact integer/decimal/fraction answers. No partial credit, marketing, billing, elaborate design system or animations. Synthetic original worksheet assets only. Demo use; no identifiable educational records. Mock OCR must be honest. Engineering decisions delegated by user; no routine clarification interviews.

## Operating priorities
Precision before coverage; human minutes saved across capture, review and corrections. This milestone adds guided capture, durable local drafts, ordered batches, retry and observed timing. Edge detection, automatic perspective/blur/glare checks and linked correction rounds remain future work.
